EV departure audit: information quality and observed service

Definition: ev-departure-audit-v1. The additive family is exported by evaluate_v2(), final KPI CSVs and the installed distribution. Each name starts with building_ev_departure_audit_ or district_ev_departure_audit_. The export manifest records this definition, population, denominators and limits.

Population and compatibility

Count actual departure events on active charger instances in the evaluated member window, including events without a target, vehicle history or observed SOC. A transition away from a connection, an identity/session change, or a declared terminal departure is an event. An open session at the end of an episode is not an invented departure. A missing schedule cannot establish an event and is outside this population.

Service and feasibility are independent questions. A departure may have known observed service but unknown feasibility, or a known physical opportunity but missing observed service. Neither unknown case becomes success or failure.

The historical *_ev_events_* and *_ev_performance_* names keep their existing definitions. In particular, their feasibility helper remains permissive when input data is missing; its battery adapters and arrival fallbacks are retained. Its invalid/non-finite interval duration now follows that same permissive rule instead of attempting an invalid replay. Do not combine historical and audited feasibility counts. The default scorecard selects the audited service ratios and also includes coverage and unknown counts. Consumers with fixed KPI lists must select the new names explicitly; exporting them does not change external jobs.

Counts and denominators

For each threshold T in target, min_acceptable, within_tolerance:

Suffix after the family prefix

Definition

departures_count

All detected departures, D.

service_observed_count

Departures with a valid target and finite observed SOC in [0, 1], O.

service_unknown_count

D − O.

service_coverage_ratio

O / D.

T_feasible_count

Confirmed individually feasible departures, F.

T_infeasible_count

Confirmed individually infeasible departures, I.

T_unknown_count

Insufficient information, U. D = F + I + U for each T.

T_coverage_ratio

(F + I) / D.

T_unknown_REASON_count

One primary reason per unknown departure; reason counts sum to U.

T_service_met_count

Observed departures meeting the service criterion, M.

T_service_observed_ratio

M / O, without filtering on feasibility.

T_feasible_service_observed_count

Confirmed feasible departures with observed service, OF.

T_feasible_service_met_count

Departures in OF that meet the service criterion, MF.

T_service_feasible_ratio

MF / OF.

Zero denominators produce None/NaN (blank in CSV), not zero or full success. District counts sum building counts; district ratios are recomputed from those counts, never averaged across buildings.

The target criterion is SOC ≥ requested SOC. Minimum acceptable service uses SOC ≥ target − ev_departure_service_tolerance. Symmetric accuracy uses abs(SOC − target) ≤ ev_departure_within_tolerance. The latter’s feasibility test concerns reaching its lower SOC threshold; it does not prove that every charging policy can stop inside the symmetric band. Existing numerical SOC tolerance is retained.

Example: four observed departures, two confirmed feasible, one infeasible and one unknown. If one feasible and the unknown departure meet the target, coverage is 3/4, observed service is 2/4, and service among confirmed feasible departures is 1/2. The unknown departure is not credited as confirmed feasible.

Missing information: exclusive reasons in precedence order

  1. missing_target: target absent, non-finite or outside supported SOC units.

  2. missing_ev: no vehicle in the connection history at departure.

  3. missing_battery: the recorded vehicle has no battery.

  4. invalid_capacity: capacity missing, non-finite or non-positive.

  5. invalid_charger_power: charger power missing, non-finite or negative. Zero power is valid: initial energy may suffice, subject to standby losses.

  6. invalid_charger_efficiency: efficiency missing or outside [0, 1].

  7. invalid_time_step: resolved charger/battery/environment interval missing, non-finite or non-positive. There is no implicit one-hour audit fallback.

  8. missing_arrival_soc: no valid boundary SOC. Use the schedule’s current SOC, an applicable arrival estimate, or initial SOC at episode index zero; never use the post-action departure SOC as its own starting evidence.

  9. unsupported_battery_model: a historical adapter does not implement the native Battery replay contract. Battery and InventoryBattery are supported.

  10. invalid_battery_parameters: native power, efficiency, standby-loss or SOC bounds are invalid, or the replay yields a non-finite SOC.

  11. outage_supply_not_replayed: the conservative replay fails during a session with an outage, and local PV/storage allocation has not been replayed.

No early success shortcut is used for a zero threshold before validating the data. Unknown means the audit cannot establish the answer, not that the simulation necessarily contains invalid data.

Window, dynamics and outage limits

Replay starts at the observed session boundary. It intersects the schedule with the connection history, so it cannot borrow charging time before a dynamic activation or from an earlier back-to-back session. A window that starts inside a session evaluates feasibility from its declared boundary SOC, not from an unobserved original arrival. Removed chargers remain available through the topology history; a replacement does not duplicate departures scheduled while that instance was inactive. Repeated evaluation does not advance the real vehicle, battery, episode, or physical trajectory.

The replay remains individual, conditioned on the existing electrical-service limits and base-load estimation. It is not a joint scheduler for competing EVs and does not prove joint service feasibility. Default model parameters are part of the declared model, not field measurements. It does not reconstruct all robustness events or solve local outage dispatch.

During an outage the audit conservatively assigns zero charging to the replay. If subsequent charging still reaches the threshold, that is sufficient for this individual test. If it does not, the result is unknown rather than a false infeasibility claim: actual local generation/storage may have served the EV. Observed departure service is still reported independently. Deferred charging that is recovered before departure is not missed service. Tests include both recovery and an actual departure shortfall with physical invariants enabled.

Validation scope

Regression cases cover each missing-data reason, analytical mixed populations, threshold differences, zero denominators, censored and partial sessions, activation boundaries, back-to-back sessions, removed chargers, reset, non-mutating evaluation, outage recovery/shortfall and CSV/manifest exports. Historical KPI tests remain part of the compatibility check. This iteration does not modify datasets, physical dispatch, rewards or market pricing, and does not claim an annual campaign or calibration against real equipment.
