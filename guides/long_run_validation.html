Continuous campaign and resource evidence

The offline long-run-audit-v1 CLI keeps runtime physical checks active throughout a continuous episode and records the exact schema, loaded-input hashes, software versions, BusinessAsUsualAgent parameters and audit-script hash. It does not change datasets, dispatch, market pricing or rewards.

Run

Run this repository script from a source checkout of v1.9.10 or later, with the required dataset available. It is not an installed PyPI console command.

python scripts/audit/audit_long_run.py \
  --schema data/datasets/pt_rec_4/evaluation/rec_4.json \
  --start 0 --transitions 35040 --block-steps 960 \
  --seed 0 --output /tmp/micro4-year2023

The output directory must be empty. The requested window must contain its terminal observation: 35,040 dispatched 900-second intervals require 35,041 observations. The CLI preserves the declared native market: internal price 80% of purchase price, zero export price. Other market contracts require their own audit.

The BAU is identified by its definition version, parameters and observation access. Declared robustness seeds and events remain enabled; --seed does not silently rewrite explicit schema seeds. The campaign is not a comparison between controllers.

What is checked

Measurements are captured after dispatch/settlement and before advancing topology to the next interval, so equipment replacement cannot rewrite an executed sample. Every executed interval checks finite reward, active member identities, measured device terminals against the member bus, excess local energy as curtailment in a grid outage, net import/export against settlement, internal energy/cash balance and native prices. Independent streaming totals are retained by member identity, including removed/re-entered members, and compared with final member/district KPIs. Appliance physical energy is reconciled with the service audit. Runtime checks independently enforce storage energy changes, SOC and equipment powers.

The final report also checks the production provenance ledger’s cumulative energy/carbon residuals, records source/destination inventories and distinguishes service coverage/unknowns from successful departures. Capacity changes are the model’s simplified degradation, not empirically validated ageing.

This CLI does not solve a second independent producer/recipient flow network at every annual interval. The independent-flow audit from iteration 4 and the physical CI matrix provide that separate, bounded evidence. The long campaign extends duration and checks aggregate/meter consistency; it does not relabel production provenance residuals as an independent provenance proof.

Files and resources

intervals.csv contains executed global indexes, active member counts, energy, cost and outage membership. summary.json is replaced atomically after each block and on completion/failure. Errors identify the failed phase and dispatch index where applicable. Progress is evidence, not a resumable environment snapshot.

kpis.csv, service.json and manifest.json accompany successful runs. Undefined EV ratios are JSON null; physical non-finite values fail the audit. Failed runs are not accepted even if they have partial exports.

RSS and peak RSS are Linux process measurements, including the interpreter, loaded inputs, runtime histories and audit overhead. Blocks record duration, active population, settlement-history length and retained provenance aggregate bytes. These distinguish expected episode-history growth from aggregate growth; they do not establish leak freedom for every configuration or reset pattern. Resource limits are measured after load/reset, at blocks and after final evaluation/export; they are not an OS-level hard memory/time reservation.

The declared campaign uses an 8,192 MiB RSS threshold and 14,400 seconds per case. The report records actual observed consumption and whether each threshold passed. Extrapolating an intermediate Premium window to a year is indicative: changes of topology, seasonal activity, allocator behaviour and final evaluation can change both time and memory. A projection is not annual validation.

Selected campaign

The prepared sequence starts with a full 2023 year in Micro-4, then a full 2023 year in Core-30 with dynamic topology/robustness, then 60 continuous days in the nominal Premium-100 configuration from global index 39,360. The latter crosses 29 February and the spring 2024 DST transition. Actual active members, not nominal configuration names, determine reported participation.

The machine-readable plan and final case reports identify precisely which stages passed, failed or were not run. Prior iteration 6 windows supply separately scoped calendar/replay evidence, including autumn 2024. No claim of continuous execution of both years in every Portuguese configuration follows from these selected runs. Empirical calibration remains iteration 8 and requires appropriate measurements.

The topology_events field counts topology log entries, including replay before the selected window. It is not a count of structural events inside that window. The campaign summary separately reports declared events inside executed indexes. Service shortfalls are reported alongside coverage; a feasible individual replay does not certify joint scheduling feasibility or policy success.
