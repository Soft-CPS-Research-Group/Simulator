Energy provenance and carbon accounting

The evaluate_v2() provenance families are building_energy_provenance_* and district_energy_provenance_*. The definition version is 1.1; the complete KPI contract version is 1.9.0. Both are present in environment metadata, DataFrame attributes and the JSON manifest accompanying exported KPIs.

Boundary and convention

The accounting boundary contains active member AC buses, stationary electrical batteries and connected vehicle batteries. It follows actual terminal transfers and changes in stored energy. Thermal tanks remain part of electrical end use; the ledger does not claim to trace heat into subsequent thermal services.

Each member’s AC bus is mixed proportionally over the control interval. Each battery is mixed proportionally and all its operations retain their order. A linear balance resolves energy charged and discharged within the same interval, including transfers between several storages. This is an accounting convention, not a claim to identify electrons or the chronology inside a sampled interval. No counter changes actions, physical SOC, degradation, rewards or prices.

Only executed intervals while a member is active contribute energy, PV, cost and carbon totals. The terminal observation is not another dispatched interval. Completed flows survive member removal and re-entry; recommissioning still starts the physical equipment from its configured initial state. The ledger classifies retired inventory as an exit and the new initial inventory as unknown input.

During an outage, PV availability that cannot serve load or charge storage is assigned to curtailment. It remains part of available generation and is excluded from useful load and instantaneous absorption. This is rejected local generation, not an external grid export. Building.pv_curtailment exposes the interval series.

PV generated after reset is the current generation cohort. Grid energy carries its acquisition carbon mass. Initial SOC and imposed arrival SOC have unknown external origin and unknown carbon, even if historically renewable. Producer identity survives local sharing and storage. The export manifest contains sparse source/producer/recipient/destination totals; no full producer-by-member time series is retained.

With financial settlement enabled, the ledger follows its actual local energy allocation, including a disabled local-trading setting. With financial settlement disabled, it uses same-interval community netting: imports are allocated with member weights and demand caps; exports are allocated proportionally. The metadata states which convention applied. No financial transaction is invented.

Metric suffixes

Prepend district_energy_provenance_ or building_energy_provenance_ to the following suffixes. Energy is in kWh, carbon mass in kgCO2 and ratios are unitless.

Suffix

Meaning

pv_generated_kwh

PV produced within the episode and active boundary.

pv_load_direct_kwh

PV delivered to electrical end use without passing through a battery. Includes direct community sharing.

pv_load_after_storage_kwh

PV delivered to electrical end use after stationary or EV storage.

pv_load_kwh

Sum of direct and after-storage useful building end use.

pv_mobility_kwh

Known PV inventory leaving in a departing connected vehicle. This is delivery to mobility, not measured trip consumption.

pv_grid_export_kwh

PV reaching the external grid, including delayed battery or V2G export.

pv_loss_kwh

PV lost in battery/charger conversion and standby.

pv_curtailment_kwh

Available PV rejected during islanded operation.

pv_stored_kwh

PV remaining inside connected/active storage. Not final consumption.

pv_asset_exit_kwh

Inventory leaving with a removed stationary asset/member.

pv_external_reset_kwh

Old inventory retired when an external SOC assignment replaces its provenance.

unknown_input_kwh

Initial inventory and imposed external SOC entering the accounting boundary.

unknown_{destination}_kwh

Energy with unknown origin at the corresponding destination.

grid_{destination}_kwh

Energy acquired from the grid at the corresponding destination.

external_import_kwh

External acquisition after the declared sharing/netting convention.

external_purchase_emissions_kgco2

External imports multiplied by the recipient’s contemporaneous carbon intensity. No export credit.

carbon_{destination}_kgco2

Known acquisition carbon carried to load, mobility, export, loss, stored stock, asset exit or external reset.

carbon_unknown_{destination}_kwh

Energy whose acquisition carbon is unknown; it is not claimed to have zero carbon.

local_import_kwh, local_export_kwh

Internal transfers, also split by pv_ and grid_ prefixes. These are not additional final destinations.

Destination substitutions are load, mobility, grid_export, loss, stored, asset_exit, external_reset and curtailment. Recipient-level PV use can exceed that member’s own generation because energy can be received from neighbours. Ratios and cohort closure are therefore published at district level:

  • pv_useful_building_ratio = pv_load_kwh / pv_generated_kwh.

  • pv_useful_with_mobility_ratio = (pv_load_kwh + pv_mobility_kwh) / pv_generated_kwh.

  • pv_balance_residual_kwh: generation minus all eight final destinations above.

  • grid_balance_residual_kwh and unknown_balance_residual_kwh: acquisition or unknown input minus the corresponding destinations.

  • carbon_balance_residual_kgco2: external acquisition emissions minus known carbon at all destinations.

  • storage_max_residual_kwh and bus_max_residual_kwh: largest reconciliation discrepancy observed during accounting.

A zero PV denominator produces an undefined ratio (None/NaN), not an artificial zero or 100%. Stored energy, losses, curtailment and asset exits are excluded from useful-use ratios. Carbon acquisition and carbon use answer different questions; do not add them together. Unknown-carbon energy is explicitly reported separately.

BAU values append business_as_usual before the unit. Differences append delta_to_business_as_usual. The sidecar uses the same ledger, boundary, configuration, timestep and definition version as the controlled simulation.

Compatibility

Existing PV self-consumption remains instantaneous absorption, which includes energy entering storage. It does not prove final useful consumption. Existing emissions remain the sum of positive member imports before sharing. These old names are retained. Definition 1.1 corrects rejected PV being counted as load or absorption during outages and excludes unexecuted/inactive intervals from totals. Recompute affected experiments; historical 1.0 totals are not interchangeable with 1.1 results. Recompute using the provenance family to answer provenance or external-boundary questions. Historical aggregated exports alone cannot reconstruct storage origins; rerun those experiments.

For example, 1 kWh PV charged and later exported gives 1 kWh of pv_grid_export_kwh, zero useful PV consumption and zero PV stock at the end. An external purchase of 1 kWh at 0.2 kgCO2/kWh gives 0.2 kgCO2 even if gross member imports before sharing total 3.5 kWh.

BESS charge/discharge and charger charge/V2G totals use gross terminal movements before netting opposing calls in one interval. Their sum is throughput; net exchange and physical dispatch retain their definitions. Legacy custom facades without gross traces retain a net-series approximation. An explicit replacement of a device terminal reading also replaces its gross trace with that signed net reading; it cannot recover unrecorded operations.

See independent balance audit for the separate reference calculation, tolerances, command and limits. Definition 1.1 and that audit are included from v1.9.7 and are absent from v1.9.6.

EV feasibility

Departure feasibility now replays the concrete battery implementation in isolated, session-sized buffers. It respects asymmetric power, maximum SOC, charger AC losses, battery curves, capacity at the beginning of the session, service headroom, deadbands and standby losses. A blocked interval does not end the search. The final connected SOC is evaluated rather than an earlier peak. The query leaves actual battery arrays, degradation, RNG and episode time intact.

This is an individual maximum-charging replay under the declared service and base-load constraints. It is not a joint schedule for all vehicles, nor a proof of global optimality for arbitrary custom non-monotone efficiency models. Legacy adapters without a native physical battery retain a compatibility approximation; missing physical/arrival data retains the legacy permissive classification.

Reproducibility and limitations

Each KPI export writes a sibling .manifest.json containing the effective schema, market settings, seed, episode window, timestep, definitions, package version, Git revision/dirty state when available, SHA-256 hashes of referenced input files, accounting totals and source/destination evidence. A wheel outside a Git checkout reports the commit as unavailable rather than borrowing an unrelated revision.

Provenance does not add electrochemical battery temperature, detailed road-trip energy, distribution power flow or field validation. Vehicle returns with imposed SOC start a new unknown cohort. Escalators remain unsupported in the entity interface; DR and dynamic topology require entity. Robustness catalogues targeting entity features are not automatically translated into flat observations. Future-data access by some reference policies and controller-quality comparisons are separate work.
